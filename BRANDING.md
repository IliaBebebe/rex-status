# REX Corp identity

Use the original metallic REX Corporation logo with the blue X accent, not a recreated wordmark, letter tile or replacement symbol.

- Canonical asset: `web/rex-corp-logo.png`, copied unchanged from REX Hub / the corporate homepage (396 × 167 transparent PNG).
- Corporate base `#07090f`, foreground `#f7f8fb`, secondary text `#aab1c3`, cyan accent `#59d6ff`, taken from the corporate homepage.
- Dark surfaces preserve the metallic logo's contrast. Amber, red, green and neutral labels communicate service state; keep these distinct from the corporate accent.
- Preserve the original logo's aspect ratio and transparency in the header, footer, page icon and sharing metadata. Brand presentation also applies to the 404 page.
- Typography retains self-hosted Manrope for readable status information. No external font or analytics requests.
- Identity styles live in `web/brand.css`; base layout, collector, incident semantics and freshness thresholds are independent of branding.
